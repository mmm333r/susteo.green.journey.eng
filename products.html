3-2. SUSTEO 51 Blended Fuel

The Cost-Effective Entry Point

SUSTEO 51 is a blend of 51% SUSTEO HVO and 49% conventional diesel fuel. This product offers a more accessible price point for companies beginning their decarbonization journey, while still delivering significant CO₂ reductions.

💡 SUSTEO 51 is classified as a biofuel product under Japanese law because it contains more than 50% biofuel content, making it eligible for sustainability reporting and green procurement programs.

💰
Lower cost barrier — more affordable than 100% SUSTEO, ideal for cost-sensitive operations

3-3. Bio-Heavy Oil

Decarbonization for Industrial Applications

Bio-Heavy Oil is a biofuel designed for industrial boilers, large marine vessels, and other heavy industrial applications that currently rely on conventional heavy fuel oil (C-heavy oil). It provides a sustainable alternative without requiring equipment replacement.

🏭 Many industrial facilities and large ships are not yet ready to switch to electricity or hydrogen. Bio-Heavy Oil provides an immediate, practical path to reducing emissions for these hard-to-abate sectors.

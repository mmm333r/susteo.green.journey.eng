ISCC Certification

Internationally Recognized Sustainability

SUSTEO holds ISCC (International Sustainability and Carbon Certification) certification, the leading international standard for sustainable biomass and biofuel products.

🌍 ISCC certification is recognized by the European Union (EU Renewable Energy Directive), the United States (RFS), Japan (Act on Sophisticated Methods of Energy Supply Structures), and other major regulatory frameworks worldwide.

What ISCC Certification Verifies:

✅ Feedstock sustainability — no deforestation, no food crop competition
✅ Full supply chain traceability from feedstock to final product
✅ Accurate GHG emission calculations using approved methodology
✅ Annual third-party audits to maintain certification
✅ Compliance with social and environmental standards

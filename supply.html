Ship Supply

Marine Vessel Supply

SUSTEO can be supplied directly to vessels at major Japanese ports. Our port facilities provide bunkering services for ships seeking to reduce their carbon footprint.

🚢 With new IMO (International Maritime Organization) regulations requiring significant emission reductions from ships, SUSTEO provides an immediate and compliant solution for marine operators.

Available at These Ports:

⚓ Yokohama Port — Kanto region
⚓ Kobe Port — Kansai region
⚓ Kure Port — Chugoku region
⚓ Kitakyushu Port — Kyushu region
🚢

Marine Supply Services

✅ Barge delivery to vessels at anchor or berth
✅ Pipeline supply at select port terminals
✅ Flexible blending ratios (20% to 100%)
✅ Certified delivery documentation for IMO reporting
✅ 24/7 scheduling for vessels in port

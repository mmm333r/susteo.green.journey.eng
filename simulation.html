How It Works

How We Calculate the Estimate

1

CO₂ Reduction Calculation

Based on ISCC-certified lifecycle GHG savings factors: SUSTEO 100% = 90% reduction, SUSTEO 51 = 45%, Bio-Heavy Oil = 80%. Applied to your fuel consumption volume.

2

Cost Estimate

Reference pricing based on current market conditions. Actual pricing varies by volume, location, and contract terms. Contact us for an accurate quote.

3

Cedar Tree Equivalent

The annual CO₂ reduction is expressed in terms of equivalent cedar trees (sugi) — one cedar tree absorbs approximately 14 kg of CO₂ per year. A helpful way to visualize your impact.

Note: All calculations are estimates based on ISCC-certified GHG savings factors and reference pricing. Actual CO₂ reduction and costs will vary based on specific feedstock, supply chain, volume discounts, and market conditions. For official sustainability reporting, use the certified values provided with your actual SUSTEO delivery documentation.

History of Challenges

From a Single Cell to Global Impact

Euglena Co., Ltd. was founded in 2005 with a mission to solve hunger, malnutrition, and environmental problems using the microalgae euglena. Our journey into biofuels began with a bold vision: that this tiny organism could help power a sustainable future.

2005
Euglena Co., Ltd. Founded
Mitsuru Izumo founded Euglena Co., Ltd. in Tokyo. The company achieved the world's first outdoor mass cultivation of euglena microalgae for food use, solving a technical challenge that had stumped researchers for decades.
2010
Biofuel Research Begins
Euglena begins exploring the potential of euglena oil as a biofuel feedstock. Research shows that euglena can produce high-quality oil suitable for biofuel production without competing with food crops.
2012
Tokyo Stock Exchange Listing
Euglena Co., Ltd. lists on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Mothers market (now TSE Growth), raising capital to accelerate biofuel research and development.
2015
World's First Euglena Biofuel Flight
A small aircraft successfully flew using biofuel made from euglena, demonstrating the viability of euglena-based aviation biofuel (SAF) for the first time in history.
2018
Yokohama Pilot Plant Established
Euglena opens a pilot plant in Yokohama to demonstrate the commercial viability of producing biofuel from used cooking oil and euglena using the HVO process. This plant becomes the foundation for SUSTEO production.
2020
SUSTEO Brand Launched
Euglena launches the SUSTEO brand, marking the transition from research to commercial biofuel product. Initial supply is limited, but the vision for nationwide distribution is established.
SUSTEO Commercialization

From Research to Reality

The commercialization of SUSTEO required not just technological breakthroughs, but the building of an entirely new supply chain — from feedstock procurement to refining, storage, and nationwide distribution.

Key Milestones in Commercialization:

Partnership with Major Energy Companies
Agreements with Kamei, Sinanen, and Hirano Sekiyu to build a nationwide supply and distribution network.
ISCC Sustainability Certification
Achieving ISCC certification validated SUSTEO's sustainability credentials for corporate buyers and sustainability reporting.
OEM Approvals
Obtaining compatibility verification from Mitsubishi Fuso and Isuzu Motors removed the final barrier to widespread commercial adoption.
12 Supply Bases Nationwide
Expanding to 12 supply locations across Japan, from Hokkaido to Kyushu, making SUSTEO accessible to companies nationwide.

Malaysia Pengerang Project

Building the Future: Malaysia Commercial Plant

To dramatically scale up SUSTEO production and reduce costs, Euglena is constructing a commercial-scale biofuel production facility in Pengerang, Johor, Malaysia — in partnership with Eni (Italy) and Petronas (Malaysia).

🇮🇹

Eni S.p.A.

Italy's major energy company. Brings HVO technology expertise and global biofuel processing know-how to the partnership.

Technology Partner
🇲🇾

Petronas

Malaysia's national oil company. Provides the Pengerang site within their integrated petroleum complex, plus local expertise and infrastructure.

Site Partner
🇯🇵

Euglena Co., Ltd.

Japan's biofuel pioneer. Leads the project development, brings sustainability certification expertise, and manages the Japanese market supply chain.

Project Lead

Project Overview

📍
Location
Pengerang Integrated Petroleum Complex (PIPC), Johor, Malaysia
🏭
Production Capacity
250,000 tons per year of HVO biofuel
🌿
Feedstock
Used cooking oil, palm fatty acid distillate (PFAD), and other sustainable feedstocks
🚢
Supply Chain
Direct sea freight to Japan and other Asian markets, leveraging Pengerang's port facilities
📅
Status
Under construction — commercial operations planned for 2025-2026

🌏 Strategic Location: Pengerang is ideally situated in Southeast Asia, close to sustainable feedstock sources and with excellent sea connections to Japan. This location enables cost-competitive production at commercial scale.

📈 Scale Impact: At 250,000 tons per year, the Pengerang plant will produce enough biofuel to reduce CO₂ emissions by approximately 700,000 tons annually — equivalent to taking 150,000 cars off the road.

🏗️

Construction Progress

~60% complete (as of 2025)

Our Vision

Our Promise for the Future

Euglena's vision is a world where biofuels are as accessible, affordable, and ubiquitous as conventional fossil fuels — enabling any company, anywhere in Japan, to make the switch to low-carbon energy.

🌿

Accessible to All

Expanding our supply network so that every company in Japan — from large corporations to small businesses — can access SUSTEO biofuel.

💰

Cost Competitive

Through the Malaysia commercial plant and economies of scale, we aim to bring SUSTEO pricing to parity with conventional diesel, removing the cost barrier to adoption.

🌍

Global Reach

Expanding beyond Japan to supply biofuel across Asia and beyond, contributing to global decarbonization goals and a healthier planet for future generations.

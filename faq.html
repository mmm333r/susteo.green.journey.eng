Equipment & Vehicles

Equipment & Vehicle Questions

Q
Can SUSTEO be used with existing diesel engines without any modifications?
▼
Yes. SUSTEO (HVO-type biofuel) is a drop-in replacement for conventional diesel fuel. It has essentially the same properties as petroleum diesel and can be used directly in existing diesel engines without any hardware modifications. Major truck manufacturers including Mitsubishi Fuso and Isuzu Motors have verified compatibility with their vehicle lineups.
Q
Will switching to SUSTEO void my vehicle warranty?
▼
SUSTEO has been verified by major vehicle manufacturers to be compatible with their diesel engines. Verified manufacturers include Mitsubishi Fuso and Isuzu Motors, who have confirmed that using SUSTEO does not void their vehicle warranties. For specific models or manufacturers not listed, please contact us and we can provide the latest compatibility information.
Q
Can SUSTEO be used in construction equipment and generators?
▼
Yes. SUSTEO has been used in a wide range of diesel equipment including excavators, bulldozers, cranes, and emergency power generators. Shimizu Corporation and Takenaka Corporation have successfully used SUSTEO in construction equipment on major building projects in Japan.
Q
How does SUSTEO perform in cold weather?
▼
SUSTEO (HVO type) has excellent cold weather performance, with a pour point of approximately -20°C. This is comparable to or better than conventional diesel fuel and significantly superior to FAME-type biodiesel, which can gel at low temperatures. SUSTEO is suitable for use in Hokkaido and other cold regions of Japan.
Q
Can SUSTEO be blended with conventional diesel in my existing tank?
▼
Yes. SUSTEO can be blended with conventional diesel at any ratio without any issues. You can start with any percentage of SUSTEO you choose. The SUSTEO 51 product is a pre-blended 51% biofuel/49% diesel blend if you prefer a ready-made intermediate option.
CO₂ Reduction

CO₂ Reduction Questions

Q
How much CO₂ can SUSTEO reduce?
▼
SUSTEO can reduce CO₂ emissions by up to 90% on a lifecycle (well-to-wheel) basis compared to conventional diesel fuel. This means that for every 100 tons of CO₂ that would have been emitted using conventional diesel, switching to SUSTEO would emit only about 10 tons. The SUSTEO 51 blended fuel achieves approximately 45% CO₂ reduction.
Q
What is "lifecycle CO₂ reduction"? Why is it calculated this way?
▼
Lifecycle CO₂ analysis (also called "well-to-wheel" analysis) measures all greenhouse gas emissions from the extraction of raw materials, through production and transportation, to the final use of the fuel. For SUSTEO, the CO₂ released when the fuel is burned was originally absorbed from the atmosphere by the plants used as feedstock — creating a near-closed carbon cycle. This internationally recognized methodology is used by ISCC certification, the EU, and Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.
Q
What about exhaust emissions other than CO₂?
▼
JARI (Japan Automobile Research Institute) testing shows that compared to conventional diesel, SUSTEO reduces particulate matter (PM) by approximately 8.5%, NOx by approximately 3.2%, and hydrocarbons (HC) by approximately 12%. This means SUSTEO improves air quality in addition to reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
Q
Is there a way to calculate how much CO₂ my company would reduce?
▼
Yes! Use our free Cost Estimation Simulator. Simply enter your monthly fuel consumption and type of equipment, and the simulator will calculate your estimated annual CO₂ reduction, cost impact, and equivalent cedar trees absorbed. No registration required.
Cost & Pricing

Cost & Pricing Questions

Q
How much does SUSTEO cost compared to conventional diesel?
▼
SUSTEO is currently priced higher than conventional diesel fuel, with the premium varying by product type, volume, location, and market conditions. SUSTEO (HVO 100%) carries the highest premium, while SUSTEO 51 (blended) is more affordable. Specific pricing is available through our supply partners after an inquiry. Use our simulator for a rough cost estimate based on your usage.
Q
Are there government subsidies available for switching to SUSTEO?
▼
Yes. The Tokyo Metropolitan Government has launched a subsidy program for companies switching to biofuels like SUSTEO. Additionally, various national and local government programs support corporate decarbonization. The availability and amount of subsidies change regularly, so please contact us for the latest information on programs applicable to your company.
Q
Can the fuel cost premium be offset by carbon credits or sustainability reporting benefits?
▼
Yes, for many companies. The additional cost of SUSTEO can be partially or fully offset by: (1) Carbon credit savings if your company has carbon pricing obligations, (2) Sustainability reporting value — customers and investors increasingly value demonstrated decarbonization, (3) Avoidance of future carbon taxes as Japan's carbon pricing framework develops, (4) Government subsidies where available. Our team can help calculate the full financial picture for your situation.
Q
Is there a minimum order volume requirement?
▼
Minimum order volumes vary by supply partner and location. Generally, delivery by tank truck is available for orders of 1,000 liters or more. Drum supply (200-liter drums) is also available for smaller users. Please contact us or your nearest supply partner for specific minimum order information.
Procurement & Supply

Procurement & Supply Questions

Q
How do I start procuring SUSTEO?
▼
The simplest way is to contact us via the form or email on this website. We will connect you with the nearest supply partner (Kamei, Sinanen, or Hirano Sekiyu), who will provide a quote, set up delivery arrangements, and handle all logistics. The process from first inquiry to first delivery typically takes 1-4 weeks.
Q
Is SUSTEO available throughout Japan?
▼
SUSTEO is currently available from 12 supply bases across Japan, from Hokkaido to Kyushu. If your location is not near an existing base, please contact us — delivery may still be possible through our partner network, and we are continuously expanding our supply network.
Q
How is SUSTEO delivered? Can it use the same tanks as conventional diesel?
▼
SUSTEO is delivered by tank truck and can be stored in existing diesel fuel tanks without any special preparation. For tanks that previously contained conventional diesel, there is no need for flushing or cleaning before switching to SUSTEO. The fuel can be used directly from your existing storage infrastructure.
Q
Can SUSTEO be supplied directly to construction sites?
▼
Yes. SUSTEO can be supplied directly to construction sites via tank truck, in the same way that conventional diesel is currently delivered. No special infrastructure is required at the site. This is how Shimizu Construction and Takenaka Corporation have been using SUSTEO at their construction sites.
Quality & Safety

Quality & Safety Questions

Q
What quality certifications does SUSTEO hold?
▼
SUSTEO holds ISCC (International Sustainability and Carbon Certification), which is the leading international standard for sustainable biofuels recognized by the EU, USA, Japan, and other major markets. Additionally, SUSTEO has been tested by the Japan Marine Survey Association (fuel quality analysis), JARI (engine performance and emissions), and verified by major vehicle manufacturers.
Q
Does SUSTEO contain any substances that could damage fuel system components?
▼
No. Unlike FAME-type biodiesel, which can degrade rubber seals and certain plastics in older fuel systems, SUSTEO (HVO type) has been confirmed by vehicle manufacturers to be fully compatible with all standard fuel system components. Long-term engine testing by Isuzu Motors showed no significant difference in engine wear compared to conventional diesel.
Q
What are the storage requirements for SUSTEO?
▼
SUSTEO has similar storage requirements to conventional diesel fuel. It has high oxidation stability and can be stored for extended periods (12+ months) without degradation under normal conditions. Standard diesel storage tanks and procedures are appropriate for SUSTEO storage. No special antioxidant additives are required.
Emissions Reporting

Emissions Reporting Questions

Q
Can SUSTEO usage be counted toward our company's CO₂ reduction targets?
▼
Yes. SUSTEO's CO₂ reduction can be counted under Scope 1 (for direct combustion in your own equipment) and Scope 3 emissions reporting frameworks including GHG Protocol and ISO 14064. The ISCC certification provides the documented GHG savings factor needed for official reporting. Euglena can provide all necessary documentation for your sustainability reporting.
Q
Can SUSTEO be used for CDP, TCFD, or SBTi reporting?
▼
Yes. SUSTEO emission reductions can be included in CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project), TCFD (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures), and SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative) reporting as verified scope 1 and scope 3 reductions. The ISCC-certified GHG savings methodology aligns with internationally recognized frameworks.
Q
What documentation is provided for emissions reporting?
▼
With each SUSTEO delivery, you receive: (1) Delivery receipt specifying volume and product type, (2) ISCC sustainability declaration confirming the GHG savings factor, (3) CO₂ reduction certificate quantifying the lifecycle emission reduction in tons of CO₂e. Euglena can also provide additional documentation for specific reporting frameworks upon request.
